Fractions as quotients,
fractions as multiplication.

Every diagram in the Unit 2 plan and the end-of-unit assessment, rebuilt as OMD JSON — area models with fractional side lengths, equal-sharing strips, and circle models. Side lengths are exact rationals, so a 3 1/4 by 4 rectangle reports an area of 13, not 12.999999999999998. The area diagrams take student input: shade them, drag them, answer them.
