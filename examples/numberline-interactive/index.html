How to Test

  • Pointer: drag dots or range handles.
  • Add: click empty space on the first line.
  • Remove: double-click a dot, or focus it and press Delete.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a dot/handle, use arrow keys, Shift+arrow for bigger jumps, Home/End for bounds.
  • Endpoint: double-click a range endpoint, or use the buttons, to cycle closed, open, and arrow.
