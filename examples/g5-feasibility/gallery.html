Feasibility · OMD × Grade 5

Can OMD render the 2025 NYST Grade 5 visuals?

Three capability gaps surfaced by the released state test, closed with new OMD components: standard algorithms with step-by-step reveal, volume with unit cubes and layer highlighting, and stacked fractions inline with problem text. All rendering is native OMD SVG — same style language as the existing library.

1 · Standard algorithms

omdAlgorithmWork — place-value grid, regrouping digits, progressive steps
Long division, revealed one step at a time (NYST 2025 Q3: find 816 ÷ 12). The highlighted row is the part the current step acts on; the step list on the right names each action.
Addition with regrouping — carry digits in red above the columns.
Subtraction across zeros — 6,320 − 2,756.
Two-digit multiplication with partial-product annotations.
Full long division with bracket, brought-down digits and quotient.

2 · Volume with unit cubes

omdRectangularPrism showUnitCubes + highlightLayer — nonstandard solids omitted
First layer highlighted (NYST 2025 Q10): “the first layer of a right rectangular prism… height is 6 unit cubes.” Volume = 5 × 3 × 6 = 90.
Top layer highlighted for “identical layers” questions (NYST 2025 Q29).
Dimension labels + inside label work over the cube grid (NYST 2025 Q36: given V = 90 ft³, find w).
All layers visible in the classic OMD prism palette.

3 · Fractions inline with text

omdMathText — [n/d] fractions and [w n/d] mixed numbers inside prose
Word-for-word from NYST 2025 Q11. Fractions are real stacked OMD math, wrapped and baseline-aligned with the sentence.
Mixed numbers (NYST 2025 Q38) — whole number + fraction as one inline token.
Constructed-response style stem (NYST 2025 Q37) mixing bold lead-ins, fractions, and wrapping at a fixed measure.
